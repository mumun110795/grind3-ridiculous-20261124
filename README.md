## About this entry

The pitch rents fake diners to empty restaurants. It sounds like a prank, but the angle is social proof: nobody walks into a silent, empty dining room, while a room that looks busy draws real, paying customers in off the street. The restaurant pays per seated stranger, and the joke hides the margin — the actors cost almost nothing to keep at the table, since they only ever order tap water and the cheapest soup. One sentence, one absurd service, one payer, and a cost line that makes the whole thing pencil out.
